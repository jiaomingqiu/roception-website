Sensory Breakthroughs

Beyond vision. How Roception is reshaping environmental interaction.

Magnetic Tactile Arrays

Our tactile sensors utilize complex magnetic field mapping, allowing grippers to measure high-resolution 3D shear and normal forces without optical occlusion.

Electro-Laser Olfaction

Combining highly sensitive electrochemical cells with precision laser spectroscopy to identify trace volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and gas signatures.

Industrial Durability

Unlike optical tactile sensors, our magnetic skins are immune to oil, dirt, and mechanical fatigue, making them perfect for harsh manufacturing environments.

Edge Sensor Fusion

On-board processing algorithms translate raw magnetic and chemical data into clean, actionable data streams for native ROS2 integration.

The Missing Sense in Humanoid AI

Vision-language models have successfully mapped the visual world, but humanoid robots remain crippled by a fundamental lack of physical understanding. Currently, there is a massive industry-wide shortage of high-fidelity touch data required to train autonomous manipulation models.

Roception is changing this. By integrating our MagTouch™ sensors into operational robotic fleets, we are actively harvesting the world's first large-scale, real-world physical interaction dataset.

  • Pioneering Datasets: Millions of annotated shear, normal force, and texture parameters.
  • Humanoid Ready: Train your Embodied AI models for complex, delicate object manipulation.
  • Data API Access: Feed continuous, physical-world interaction data directly into your neural networks.
